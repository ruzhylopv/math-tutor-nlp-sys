# Plan: M5 `hint`, counterexample tool, leakage check, abstention (approved 2026-10-27)

Goal: a graded Socratic hint grounded in the misconception, that never reveals the answer. Roadmap:
`docs/plans/design/2026-10-25-roadmap.md`. M1-M4 are merged.

## Decisions (from the developer)
- The wrong step is stored at diagnosis time in a field `parse_input` does not reset (option (a)); `hint` does not
  read `student_steps`, `focus_step` or `step_verdicts`. Rejected: (b) clearing the diagnosis on a message without
  working (a follow-up question would lose the misconception), (c) resetting only on a new attempt (a stale attempt
  stays in state).
- Abstention (a minimum retrieval score) and stale `topic`/`level` are settled in M5. The out-of-scope subject policy
  stays in M6 (orchestrator behaviour).
- Eval data: option A, cases reused from `retrieve.jsonl` (about 40 per split across categories, plus all
  hand-written cases). No new hand-written cases.

## Layers (a stack of PRs, lowest first)
1. **State and diagnosis context.** `state.py`: `ErrorContext(step_number, before, after, correct_steps)` and
   `error_context: ErrorContext | None`; `topic` and `level` become optional. `finish_diagnosis` writes `error_context`
   from `focus_step` and `student_steps` (None on a clean solution, so a stale value cannot survive a new diagnosis;
   it stays a deterministic node with unit tests only). Rejected: writing it in `localize` (does not run on the clean
   path). `parse_input` sets `topic` and `level` to None when the extracted problem differs from the stored one.
2. **Abstention in `retrieve`.** `MIN_SCORE`: below it `misconception` is None (candidates stay for the log). The value
   is picked on the dev score distribution; the coverage/precision trade-off goes in the retrieve eval.
3. **Tools.** `tools/counterexample.py` (`counterexample(before, after, problem)`: first small integer where the two
   lines differ in SymPy, never a solution of the original problem; None for inequalities and unparsable input);
   `tools/leakage.py` (`find_leaks(text, problem, before)`: `var = <solution>` and any expression SymPy-equal to
   `expand`/`factor`/`simplify` of `before`); `tools/canned_hints.py` (one fixed hint per level for the M6 fallback).
4. **`hint` node and prompt.** `prompts/hint.md`, structured output `HintText` via `call_structured`. Levels: 1
   Socratic question, no numbers; 2 the tool's counterexample, worded by the LLM; 3 the misconception named in tutor
   voice, no corrected expression; 4 the general `correct_rule`, asking to redo the step. Every level praises the earlier
   correct steps first; none gives the corrected line or the answer. Cap 4. Writes `hint_text`, `stage="hinted"`. The
   leakage check, retry and canned fallback stay in the M6 orchestrator.
5. **Eval and docs.** `evals/scripts/eval_hint.py`: leakage rate (auto), counterexample validity (auto), a rubric
   judge (`hint_judge.md`, structured pass/fail per criterion: praise, Socratic, on target, level-appropriate), and a
   human check of about 20 sampled hints. Dev with `--limit 15`; the test split once at the end. Docs: `decisions.md`,
   `crisp_dm_log.md`, close the architecture OPEN notes.

## Open points
- The judge is the same free model that writes the hints (lenient on itself); the human check is the counterweight.
- No new dependencies expected.
